🇺🇸 US Market & Macroeconomic Analysis

See whether a stock went up or down along with the US stock market. Compare it with technology stocks, bonds, and the dollar to understand what might be affecting its price.

Sources: historical ETF prices via the app's public market-data feed. SPY tracks the S&P 500, QQQ the Nasdaq-100, IWM US small caps, TLT long-duration Treasury bonds, UUP the US dollar, XLE energy stocks and XLK technology stocks. ETFs are imperfect proxies: TLT is not a direct Treasury-yield quote, UUP is not the DXY index, and ETF changes are not macroeconomic releases (CPI, GDP, jobs or Fed decisions). Data may be delayed or unavailable.

⚙ Display & Privacy

🔑 Personal Finnhub API Key

The website owner can configure a common public Finnhub key in config.js so all visitors automatically access stock quotes, company search, profiles, metrics and headlines. The key is saved only in this browser's localStorage, not in GitHub. It is accessible to browser scripts and visible in network requests to Finnhub; use only on a trusted device.

A shared key in config.js takes priority over a personal key. WARNING: The shared key is visible to every visitor and in the public GitHub repository. Without a key, the limited public price feed is used. If you previously shared your key, rotate it before saving here.
